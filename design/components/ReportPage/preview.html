Where we are going Updated

Every field crew should plan the job, do the work and prove it was done in one place, even with no signal. By the end of 2027 we want 12,000 crews to open Fieldbook every week, up from 4,100 today.

Most customers still stitch the day together from spreadsheets, group chats and paper forms. Fieldbook replaces the stitching, so operations leads stop chasing updates and start trusting the record.

Who we build for in Healthcare

Home health and home care agencies with 50 to 1,500 clinicians. The buyer is the director of operations; the daily user is the clinician on the road.
