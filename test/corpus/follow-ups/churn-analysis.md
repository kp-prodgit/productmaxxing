# PMX-88: Why starter accounts leave in month two

## Summary

Starter accounts churn most in their second month, after the first credits run out [T2][C1].

## Findings

- Credits run out around day 20 for half of starter accounts [T2].
- The upgrade prompt shows only after a failed render [C1].

## Recommendations

- Show the upgrade prompt at 80% of credits used. **PM decides:** whether to ship it to every starter account or test it on half first.
- Send the top-up offer by email three days before credits run out.
- **PM decides:** Check the top-up email copy with legal before it goes out.

- When does the billing team plan to change the invoice date?
- Which message got the most upgrades in the spring test? Ask the growth lead.
- What would a credit rollover cost per account?
