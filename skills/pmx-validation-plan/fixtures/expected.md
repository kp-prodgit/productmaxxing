# Validation plan for automatic offboarding

## Hypothesis

Security owners at multi-workspace customers will pay for a pilot that removes leavers from every workspace automatically [T2] [T3].

## Who we ask

- The customer that offered budget this quarter [T3]
- The customer with the audit finding [T2]

## The ask

A paid 60-day pilot covering all of the customer's workspaces, converting to an annual subscription. Price options: a flat pilot fee or the first quarter of the subscription. **PM decides**.

## What counts

Interest → intro to the security owner → security review started → paid pilot signed → subscription. A signed paid pilot from one of the two customers counts as validated.

## The buyer's path

- A security review of about three weeks before any new vendor [T3]. We'll need a security questionnaire and a data-flow description.
- Security signs and holds the budget [T3].
- *Assumption:* legal needs a DPA; confirm on the first call.

## Kill criteria

No security review started at either customer within 30 days of the build being ready.

## Open questions

- Pilot price. **PM decides**.
- Whether to ask a third customer now or wait for the first result. **PM decides**.
- Does the customer's legal team need a DPA before the pilot? — ask: the account lead
