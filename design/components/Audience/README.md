Who sees a section, in words. It replaces the row of five squares.

**Use** `.aud.some` with the lock wherever a section is restricted, naming the groups: "ELT only", "ELT and Engineering". When all but one group see it, name the exception: "Everyone except Sales". `.aud.draft` for a draft. In a list, a section everyone sees shows **no chip at all**; use the `.aud` "Everyone" form only where the audience is the subject (the audience control itself, a release summary).

**You provide** the group names as the product team spells them, joined with "and" for two and commas for three. More than three: "4 of 5 groups", with the names on focus and in the accessible name.

- Do put it at the right edge of the row it describes, on the title's baseline.
- Don't abbreviate groups to codes, and don't give groups colours.
- Don't show it on every row. The lock is information because most rows have none.
