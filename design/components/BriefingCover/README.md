The opening of every page a group reads. It answers five questions before the reader scrolls: what is this, what is the one thing to remember, who is it for, how current is it, and what changed.

**Use** it once at the top of a briefing, in the briefing register. Never in the studio or the Workspace.

**You provide** the team and the audience ("For Sales"), a confidentiality line when the page is restricted, the title as `h1`, a standfirst of at most three lines, the edition and date, the owner by name, and the change list (`.b-changes`) with `.mark` New or Updated and a link to each passage. On a first edition the change list is omitted, not replaced by "First edition."

- Do write the standfirst as the takeaway, not a description of the document.
- Do name a person. A page with an owner gets read.
- Don't open with a table, a status matrix or the tool's own state. Pipeline state belongs to the author, not the reader.
- Don't use an all-capitals kicker; the `.for` line does that job in sentence case.
