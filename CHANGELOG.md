# Changelog

Versions follow [semantic versioning](https://semver.org): new features raise the minor number, fixes the patch number, and anything that breaks an older CLI, file or team the major number. See "Updating" in the README.

## 0.39.0: Names stay out of Linear, accepts reach your team, and clearer follow-ups

- **A customer's data room no longer holds a whole thread.** In the end-to-end test one investor line ("Initech Ventures wants the cost model in the data room by Friday") and a customer's security review ("Globex's procurement set up a data room for our security docs") held a whole Slack thread as "2 passages are about fundraising", so its pricing decision and a GPU cost update never reached team work, and a new task went to Linear with the old figure. Now a data room is fundraising only with the raise near it: an investor, a fund, a round, Series A or B, a valuation, a term sheet or one of your team's confidential contacts in its sentence or the one before, or "our" or "the" data room in a meeting or thread with fundraising elsewhere. A customer's, partner's or procurement's data room never is: a security review, vendor docs, a customer's acquisition, or due diligence on you as a vendor. And fundraising holds a whole meeting or thread only when it's mostly about it, two or more passages that make up at least a third of it; otherwise just those passages, and a next sentence with their figures, are cut and the rest is used. A title about fundraising and a meeting with a confidential contact are still held whole, and Linear still never gets a sentence that mentions a data room. Every Mac checks its shared quotes again at its next background run. (B2, PRIV-2)
- **An accept reaches your team's repo even when a teammate's work got there first.** On a team repo that isn't on GitHub, accepting a draft while the team's repo had newer work kept it on your Mac, and the `git push` the answer suggested was refused, since your Mac was behind: it took `git pull`, then `git push`. Now PMX puts your accept on top of the team's newer work and pushes it, fast-forward only and never forced, and your checkout takes that newer work too. Your unsaved edits stay as they are. (REV-4)
- **When PMX can't share an accept, it leaves everything as it was and tells you what to run.** If your accept clashes with the team's newer work, or the team's repo can't be reached, nothing is changed anywhere and the accept still stands. The answer says why; Needs you, `pmx status` and `pmx doctor` (a warning, no longer ✔) say it too, with the exact command, such as `git -C '<repo>' pull --rebase origin main && git -C '<repo>' push origin main`. (REV-4, JOB-10)
- **The background run shares accepts that never reached the team, and follows the team again.** Once an accept stayed on a Mac, every background run said "couldn't fast-forward main… working from what's checked out", so that Mac stopped taking the team's settings, skills and minimum PMX version, with nothing in status or doctor. Now each run puts that Mac's accepts on top of the team's work and pushes them, including accepts made before 0.38.0 that were never pushed, then takes the team's changes as usual. (JOB-10, REV-4)
- **PMX never pushes your own commits.** A push sends every commit your main has, so PMX pushes automatically only when all of them are its own: accepts and settings applies, which it knows by their message (`Accept pmx/…`). If you committed something of your own on main, PMX pushes nothing and Needs you asks you to push, naming your commits. (REV-4, JOB-10)
- **Linear doesn't point at a team repo that hasn't got the draft.** When an accept stays on your Mac, its comment says "Accepted by ana. The accepted draft is on ana's Mac until it's pushed to the team repo." An accept that reached the team still says only who accepted it. (REV-4)
- **Follow-ups from an accepted draft say what they mean.** A call that leaned on a word lost what it stood for ("Decide whether it is a separate line or bundled", "Decide whether to commission that test before Dec 8"). Now it says, when the item's label or the sentence before does ("Decide whether the dedicated-capacity price is a separate line or bundled", "Decide whether to commission the pricing test before Dec 8"), and otherwise starts with the item's label ("Price floor: decide whether to commission that test before Dec 8"). PMX never guesses. A yes/no call reads "Decide whether the monthly allowance is pooled across workspaces, or per seat", not "Decide: Is the monthly allowance pooled …?", and "Do we have another email integration planned?" is "Decide whether another email integration is planned". A follow-up task's title that leaned on "this" or "it" does the same, or starts with the item's label or the task's subject ("Globex integration: does the integration need this for the contract?"). A question to ask keeps its words and order, with a capital, its "?" and no filler like "exactly" ("Ask the security lead: What will the risk committee accept as proof?"); in an older draft without marks, a group the question names is who to ask ("Ask Globex's procurement: …", "Ask the security team: …"), never a person; and when the draft says nothing more about it, its to-do says "PMX-39's accepted draft lists this as an open question." instead of "The draft leaves it open." A company's name keeps its capital: "Decide on the Globex renewal terms", not "the globex renewal terms". (FOLLOW-1)
- **Linear never gets a name the words themselves give.** `pmx move <to-do> product` queued "Ask Dana which Globex clients are billed through them…" for Linear: every meeting it came from started a sentence with Dana, so PMX never learned she was a person. Now PMX also reads the words going to Linear: a name right after "ask", "tell", "thanks", "cc", "per", an @mention and the like is someone's, whatever the meetings say, so the move is refused with the reason and the to-do stays yours, even one you typed. A company or product your meetings show still goes: "Ask Globex for the list" moves. With the meetings read, "follow up with Dana" and "confirm the list with Dana" count too. It's the same check everywhere PMX writes to Linear: suggested tasks, approving, moves, follow-ups and the draft-ready comment. (PRIV-6, TODO-5)
- **A name your meetings only start sentences with is still a name.** "Dana knows which is which" and "Dana on our side has the export" now tell PMX that Dana is a person, and so do "Lee from Globex" and "Avery at Initech". What a company does as often doesn't count ("Globex wants SSO", "Initech asked for the log"), and nor does a word your meetings also write as a company or product ("the Globex team"). A task waiting for you that an earlier PMX checked, whose meetings are no longer on your Mac, now waits for you to drop it or keep it as a to-do rather than going as it is. (PRIV-6)
- **A to-do keeps its meetings' check through every move.** A to-do whose meetings agents can't quote, moved to the team, taken back and moved again, lost the run it came from, so the second move checked its words against your CRM and do-not-read lists only. Now the task names that run, and nothing of its meetings ("Sources: none agents can quote", with the `pmx explain` line), taking it back keeps it, and moving it again checks its words against everyone those meetings name. (TODO-5, PRIV-6)
- **A follow-up's kept quotes no longer cut good sentences.** A follow-up waiting for you keeps a copy of its original task's shared quotes, with the names already swapped out. Approving it read names out of that copy anyway, so a product called "Copper Lantern" could pass for a person and its sentence was left out of Linear. Now the copy remembers its names were swapped, and approving reads none from it, as from your team's shared quotes. (RUN-5, PRIV-6)
- **A company of two words or an "&" goes to Linear too.** "Ask Initech Partners for the list…" was refused as naming a person, since PMX knew the company only as its email domain's name, "initechpartners". Now a company after "ask", "thanks" and the like matches however it's written: "Initech Partners", "Hart & Stone" or "Hart and Stone" for hartstone.example, and "Initech" alone for initechpartners.example. A to-do you type, with no meeting behind it, also knows the companies your other meetings and threads show and the companies on your CRM list, so "Ask Hart & Stone for the signed order form" moves; "Thanks Noor for the order form" still doesn't. (PRIV-6, TODO-5)
- **Reword a to-do PMX didn't send.** The refusal said "Reword the to-do and move it again", but nothing could reword one, and typing it again lost its meetings. Now `pmx todo edit <id> "<new words>"` changes its words and keeps everything else (its meetings, its due date, where it sits), and the refusal names that command. A to-do in Linear as your own comes back first (`pmx move <id> private`), since its words are its title there. (TODO-5)
- **A to-do keeps its due date through every move.** A to-do due Nov 30, moved to the team and taken back, came back with no date. Now the date the task says at the top ("…from their own to-dos, due 2026-11-30.") comes back with it, as a to-do in Linear as your own or a private one. (TODO-5)
- **Your own to-dos in Linear are checked like every other Linear text.** With sending your own to-dos to Linear on, `pmx todo linear <id>`, Send to Linear on PMX's page and `pmx move <id> todo` sent a to-do's words as they were, names included. Now they go through the same check as a move to the team: a name its words give or its meetings show, words repeated from a meeting, your CRM and do-not-read lists, fundraising, confidential contacts and contact details. One that fails stays your private to-do, nothing reaches Linear, and the answer, on the page too, says why in one line with the `pmx todo edit` command that rewords it. (SIG-7, PRIV-6, TODO-5)
- **Holidays and greetings aren't names.** "Happy Thanksgiving to the three of us" became "[PERSON-2] to the three of us" in a task's shared quotes. Now a holiday ("Thanksgiving", "Christmas", "Labor Day", "Lunar New Year") or a greeting ("Happy …", "Merry …", "Cheers") is never read as a name, so shared quotes keep it as written and Linear text with it isn't cut. That goes for other languages too: "Feliz Navidad", "Joyeux Noël", "Bonne Année", "Frohe Weihnachten", "Buon Natale", "Feliz Año Nuevo", "Chinese New Year". (RUN-5, PRIV-6)
- **A fact a later call says again in other words is in a task once, and nothing else in its sentence is lost.** When a later call joined a task waiting for you, or when you approved one, PMX left out a sentence that said again what one already there said only when both named a company from the task's Sources line and shared a date or figure, or asked the same question, so most restatements in real merged tasks stayed twice. A summary's sentence often carries two facts ("A plan for faster retrains is owed to Acme, whose close slipped past GA to Jan 29."), so PMX now compares sentences clause by clause too: a clause said again is left out and the rest joins on its own ("Acme will not sign before it has an answer." of "…, and its close date moved to Jan 29."), and a clause that says one already there more fully takes its place in that sentence ("Hooli's foundry licence does not cover server-side rendering, and …", "Vandelay, a $360k pilot, will use the beta …, so the spec …"). The company can come from the sentences themselves ("Hooli's", "Globex Hotels", "owed to Acme"), since a Sources line often names none ("3 threads in #competitive-intel"); two sentences about the same thing that share most of their words (at least five, 70% of the shorter one's) are one fact, with or without a date; and "moved", "slipped", "pushed" and "delayed" count as one word. When one of two sentences says all the other says and more, that one stays, in the other's place, and `pmx proposals` and PMX's page say so ("said 1 sentence of the description more fully: …"). A new fact always joins, however many words it shares: a different date or figure ("14-property" beside "105-seat"), a date or figure no sentence there states, a claim turned around ("Hooli wants SSO" and "Hooli doesn't want SSO"), another company, another question ("whether …" and "how …"), another distinctive term (an acronym, a product or plan name, or "server-side" beside "client-side": "lists SSO" and "lists SCIM" are two rows, which the old repeat check merged), and a short sentence with only two words in common ("Acme renewed in March" and "Acme churned in March"). (INPUT-2)

- **The background job finds Claude Code wherever your shell does.** `pmx init` saves the full path to Claude Code in the job and puts its folder on the job's PATH, so an npm install under nvm no longer fails with `spawn claude ENOENT`. Doctor fails when the job can't find Claude Code. Run `pmx init` again to fix a job registered by an older pmx.
- **A job that keeps failing shows (OBS-2).** `pmx status` shows the job's last pass, and doctor warns after three failed passes in a row, with the first error and the log's path.
- **"Set up" tasks that don't run show (CLAUDE-4).** `pmx schedule` and doctor show each task's last run. They warn when a run started and didn't finish, which usually means it waits for an approval nobody saw (the app skips later runs meanwhile), and when a task's scheduled time passed with no run. Runs from before this version left no record, so that clock starts at the update. `pmx schedule` also says where the task files are.
- **An unsupported Node stops the install,** with the steps in order: install Node 24 (Homebrew, or `nvm install 24 && nvm alias default 24`), open a new Terminal, install pmx again, run `pmx init`. Doctor gives the same steps.
