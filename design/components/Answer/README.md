The first thing on every screen: one sentence that says where things stand, and the one button that moves them on.

**Use** it once, at the top of the work area, above everything else. The sentence is written from state, not from a template of labels: say what is true and whose move it is.

**You provide** an optional eyebrow (the team or the place), the sentence as an `h1`, at most one supporting line, and in `.act` the next step as `.btn.pri` with at most one quiet button beside it.

- Do write a whole sentence a person could repeat: "Nothing is waiting on you. Everything released is up to date."
- Do keep it under 14 words; put the rest in the supporting line.
- Don't use a noun as the title ("Home", "Releases"). The rail already says where the person is.
- Don't show a count instead of a sentence ("Waiting on you 3").
- When nothing is asked, leave `.act` empty. Zero filled buttons is correct then.
