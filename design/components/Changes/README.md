The list that closes a changed chapter: what the change means for the person reading, one bold lead-in and one sentence per item, with square brand bullets.

**Use** `.rp-changes` at the end of a chapter that changed since the reader's last edition, after the comparison table. Leave it out of a first edition and of a chapter with no change; the normal case is silent.

**You provide** an `h3` "What this changes for" and the group name, then up to six `li`, each a `b` lead-in that names the thing (a section, a figure, a roadmap entry) and one sentence saying what is now true or what to do. Order by consequence: withdrawn and held back first, then moved figures and dates, then new and updated sections. A sixth item may say how many more changes the appendix lists.

- Do write from the data: a figure's old and new value, an entry's old and new window.
- Do say what to do when the reader must act ("Do not quote it before 4 Dec").
- Don't invent benefits or outcomes the data does not state.
- Don't use the brand colour for the lead-in; bullets carry the brand.
