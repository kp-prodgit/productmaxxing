What a reader sees in place of a passage that is held back or withdrawn. It keeps the passage's number, so "look at four" still means the same thing.

**Use** `.b-hold` in the exact place of the passage it replaces, with the same `.no`. A withdrawn passage whose place is gone goes at the end of its chapter.

**You provide** the number, the passage title as `h2` when the title itself may be read, and a `.note` with `role="note"`. Held back: `.k` with the pause icon and "Being revised since 14 Nov. Do not quote or send it.", then "Next update by" a date, and an optional `.why` saying who is doing what. Withdrawn: `.k` with the cross and "Withdrawn on 16 Nov.", the line "Do not quote or send the earlier wording.", and the approved replacement in a `.say` headed "Say this instead".

- Do give the date the reader can expect more. Every wait says who has the next move.
- Do put the instruction in the first line. A reader skimming before a call must not miss it.
- Don't show the held wording, struck through or faded.
- Don't renumber the passages that follow.

States: held back, held back with a note, withdrawn with wording to say instead.
