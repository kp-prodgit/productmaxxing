Where we are going

By the end of FY28, every crew that visits a site plans the job in Fieldbook first and records what it found before it leaves. We win when the plan and the record are the same thing.

What this demands of the platform

ELT and Engineering

Changed since edition 1

Offline capture has to be as reliable as online. That means a sync engine we own, conflict rules a crew lead can understand, and photos that survive a dead battery.

What we will not do

We do not build field hardware. We do not do the field work ourselves. We do not sell to crews of fewer than five, because they plan on paper and are right to.
