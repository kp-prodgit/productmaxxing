The top of page one of a report: who made it, what it is, what it is for, who prepared it and when, closed by one thick rule in the brand colour.

**Use** `.rp-cover` once, first on the first sheet, before section 1. The sheets after it carry the running header instead.

**You provide** the wordmark in `.wm` (the team name set in type until a workspace has a logo), the title as `h1`, a `.purpose` line of one sentence saying what this edition is for, and a `.by` line: "Prepared by" the person and role in `b`, then the month and year. The rule is drawn by the component in `--brand`.

- Do write the title as what it is and who it is for: "Product briefing for Sales · Healthcare".
- Do make the purpose line a reason to read, with the date the content is current to.
- Don't add a second rule, a picture or a table to the cover; section 1 follows straight after.
- Don't set the wordmark in the brand colour; brand never colours text.

States: default brand, workspace brand (inline `--brand`).
